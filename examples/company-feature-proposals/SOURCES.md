# Company provenance and historical outcomes

Research access date: **2026-09-22**. This file is evaluator-side provenance, not a proposal input or a feature-verdict answer key.

The corpus uses five **scaled commercial businesses** (C01, C02, C03, C06, C07) and five **historically failed or shut-down businesses** (C04, C05, C08, C09, C10). These are dated sampling categories: commercial scale does not imply uninterrupted profitability, product quality, or an attractive stock or crypto investment. Failure includes bankruptcy or an agreed wind-down where specified below; it does not necessarily mean that every asset, successor, or legal entity ceased to exist. Acquisition alone is not classified as failure.

Company names make these examples **not truly blinded**. Retrospective disclosures and later outcomes must not be treated as evidence available before a failure. The failed-company proposals are fictional counterfactuals, not authentic recovered internal plans or leakage-free historical backtests. Undated help pages describe capabilities observed at access time; do not backdate them to the financial reporting periods below.

Only the explicitly sourced company facts are real-world evidence. Proposed feature metrics, personnel allocations, controls, prices, budgets, customer observations, and experiments are synthetic scenario material, not claims about these companies. No source establishes demand for a proposed feature, proves it never existed, or determines its Idea Autopsy verdict. No comprehensive novelty search was performed.

## C01 — Slack

**Category:** Scaled commercial business, measured at FY2021. Slack reported $902.6 million annual revenue and more than 156,000 paid customers, alongside a GAAP operating loss. This supports commercial adoption, not a blanket profitability claim. [Slack Announces Strong Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2021 Results](https://slack.com/blog/news/slack-announces-strong-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2021-results), published **2021-03-04**, period ended **2021-01-31**.

**Product basis:** Channel-based conversations and access-dependent visibility are documented in [What is a channel?](https://slack.com/help/articles/360017938993-What-is-a-channel), undated help page, accessed **2026-09-22**.

## C02 — Shopify

**Category:** Scaled commercial business, measured at FY2024. Shopify reported $8.9 billion revenue, up 26%, with subscription-solutions and merchant-solutions revenue streams. [Shopify FY2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594805/000159480525000012/shop-20241231.htm), signed **2025-02-11**, period ended **2024-12-31**.

**Product basis:** Shopify documents customer-initiated return requests and states that exchanges cannot be requested through self-serve returns. [Setting up self-serve returns and cancellations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/returns/self-serve-returns/setup), undated help page, accessed **2026-09-22**. This is an access-time limitation, not a claim about every historical or third-party Shopify workflow.

## C03 — Zoom

**Category:** Scaled commercial business with positive operating profitability in the cited period. Zoom reported FY2025 revenue of $4,665.4 million and a 17.4% GAAP operating margin. [Zoom FY2025 Financial Results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1585521/000158552125000027/zm-20250224ex991.htm), published **2025-02-24**, period ended **2025-01-31**.

**Product basis:** Host-initiated meeting summaries use speech-to-text data and can be shared with participants. [Using Meeting Summary with AI](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0058013), undated help page, accessed **2026-09-22**. Existing summary functionality does not establish demand for a proposed extension.

## C04 — Everpix

**Category:** Historical shutdown. The photo-collection company's retrospective dataset records a shutdown announcement on **2013-11-05** and subscription sales sufficient for variable costs but not fixed costs. [Everpix-Intelligence](https://github.com/everpix/Everpix-Intelligence), company-published dataset frozen **2013-11-06**; publication date not stated.

**Product and outcome basis:** Its [Timeline & Numbers](https://github.com/everpix/Everpix-Intelligence/blob/master/Timeline%20%26%20Numbers.md) records Memories email in May 2013, Flashback in June 2013, and shutdown in **December 2013**. These retrospective sources do not establish that a proposed feature would have prevented closure.

## C05 — Rdio

**Category:** Bankruptcy and service closure. [Pandora's FY2015 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1230276/000123027616000057/p-12312015x10k.htm), signed **2016-02-18**, confirms bankruptcy proceedings in November 2015 and Rdio service discontinuation on **2015-12-22**.

**Product basis:** [Pandora to acquire key assets from Rdio](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1230276/000123027615000046/p-ex991_20151116.htm), published **2015-11-16**, identifies streaming-music technology and IP assets, explicitly distinguishing them from the operating business. Acquisition itself is not the failure marker. These sources do not verify rights or licensing for a hypothetical new listening feature.

## C06 — Coinbase

**Category:** Scaled commercial business with positive reported net income in the cited period. Coinbase reported FY2024 revenue of $6.6 billion and net income of $2.6 billion. [Coinbase Q4 and FY2024 Shareholder Letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1679788/000167978825000021/q424shareholderletter.htm), filed **2025-02-13**, period ended **2024-12-31**. These company-wide results do not establish feature-level economics or future returns.

**Product basis:** Coinbase Prime documents consensus and transfer-approval policies. [Prime security settings](https://help.coinbase.com/en/prime/securing-your-account/security-settings), undated help page, accessed **2026-09-22**.

## C07 — Kraken

**Category:** Company-reported commercial scale. Kraken reported **2.5 million funded accounts** at the end of 2024. [Kraken 2024 financials](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-2024-financials), dated **2025-01-31**, accessed **2026-09-22**. This is company-reported adoption, not a claim about investment returns or audited profitability.

**Product basis:** Kraken documents separately selectable API permissions, including Query Ledger Entries. [How to create an API key on Kraken Pro](https://support.kraken.com/articles/how-to-create-an-api-key-on-kraken-pro), last updated **2025-03-31**, accessed **2026-09-22**. Permission availability is not evidence of customer demand for a proposed reporting feature.

## C08 — FTX

**Category:** Historical exchange failure. The [CFTC's historical record](https://www.cftc.gov/About/HistoryoftheCFTC/history_2020s.html), entry for **2022-11-11**, records bankruptcy for FTX Trading and most affiliates. The SEC's [2022 enforcement announcement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2022-219), published **2022-12-13**, describes the trading platform. Statements of wrongdoing in that complaint are allegations, not independently adjudicated findings.

**Separate adjudicated outcome:** The DOJ's [Bankman-Fried sentencing announcement](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/samuel-bankman-fried-sentenced-25-years-prison), published **2024-03-28**, reports his conviction and sentencing for fraud involving customer funds. A hypothetical reconciliation or transparency feature must not be assumed to establish solvency, segregation, or prevention of managerial misconduct.

## C09 — Celsius

**Category:** Historical crypto-lending failure. The FTC's [Celsius settlement and charges announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/07/ftc-reaches-settlement-crypto-platform-celsius-network-charges-former-executives-duping-consumers), published **2023-07-13**, describes its lending/deposit product context and **July 2022** bankruptcy.

**Qualification:** A settlement announcement and allegations against former executives are not interchangeable with adjudicated findings. This source is retrospective, not pre-bankruptcy evidence. No hypothetical withdrawal, liquidity, or risk-disclosure feature is represented as making customer claims safe, guaranteeing repayment, or resolving the company's underlying financial condition.

## C10 — Terraform Labs

**Category:** Historical business failure and agreed wind-down. The SEC's [Terraform and Do Kwon settlement announcement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-73), published **2024-06-13**, describes UST, its **2022** collapse, Terraform's **2024-01-21** bankruptcy filing, and a **June 2024** wind-down agreement.

**Qualification:** The token collapse, company bankruptcy, and wind-down agreement are distinct events. The announcement of an agreement is not proof that every wind-down step was completed that day. A hypothetical monitoring or simulation feature does not prove that UST's stabilization mechanism would have remained solvent or maintained its peg.
